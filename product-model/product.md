# FocusForge

FocusForge helps people block distractions, build a study routine and see completed effort become progress. The Android app combines subject-based focus timers, app boundaries, daily goals, achievements and optional private study groups and weekly leaderboard sharing.

## Intent

“Less scrolling. More studying.” FocusForge exists to “block distractions, build a study routine, and keep track of the effort that turns into progress.” — README.
