# Subject

A named area of study or work chosen by the learner.

## Information kept

- **Name** — Distinct name within the local catalogue.
- **Appearance** — The chosen or suggested identifying symbol and colour.
- **Weekly target** — Desired study hours per local Monday-to-Sunday week.
- **Today minutes** — Completed minutes starting today.
- **Week hours** — Completed hours starting this local week.
